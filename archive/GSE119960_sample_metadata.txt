Characteristics:

  - genotype: WT
  - tissue: hypothalamus
  - age: 8 weeks
  - barcode position: Bases 1 to 12 on R1
  - umi position: Bases 13 to 20 on R1
Description: Sample 1
Extract protocol:
  - Single-cell suspensions were processed through Drop-Seq to generate single-cell cDNA libraries attached to microbeads. Microbeads were counted, and amplified by PCR, and the 3' end of the cDNA prepared for sequencing using a modified Nextera XT protocol.
  - Mouse hypothalamus was dissected and dissociated into single-cell suspension, followed by drop-seq library preparation. Libraries were prepared following the latest drop-seq protcol.
Data processing:
  - Base calls performed with bcl2fastq2.15.
  - Read 2 was aligned with Star v2.5.0c.
  - Each aligned read was tagged with its mate-pair UMI and cell barcode.
  - Each aligned exonic read was tagged with a gene name.
  - A digital gene expression matrix was built by counting the number of unique UMIs per gene within each cell; columns are cells, and rows are genes.
  - Samples 1-6 -- prepared from both WT and IDOL KO mouse hypothalami on multiple separate days -- were merged together into a single matrix. Merged DGE file from all 6 samples generated. Entries contain raw UMI counts.
  - Genome_build: mm10 (GRCm38)
  - Supplementary_files_format_and_content: Digital gene expression matrices (genes x cells). Compressed, flat text files with tab delimiters.
