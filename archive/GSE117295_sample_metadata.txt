[1/13] GSM3290582

Title: CT14
Source name: suprachiasmatic nucleus
Organism: Mus musculus
Molecule: polyA RNA
Characteristics:
  - strain: C57BL/6
  - tissue: suprachiasmatic nucleus
  - age: 8 weeks
  - sampling enviroment: constant dark
Extract protocol:
  - Mice were sacrificed by acute cervical dislocation. Mice heads were chilled in the ice-cold dissection buffer for 10 seconds immediately. Brains were dissected out from the chilled head rapidly. The SCN region were microdissected in ice-cold dissection buffer by microsurgery forceps and knives according to the mice brain atlas . The SCN region tissues from 5 mice were pooled together. Tissues were digested by papain for 45 min and DNase I for 3min in digestion buffer with bubbling (95% O2, 5%CO2) under 37℃ incubation. Digestion was neutralized by adding 2ml digestion neutralizing buffer (KCl 5.33 mM, NaHCO3 26.19 mM, NaCl 117.24 mM, NaH2PO4 1.01mM, Dextrose 5.56mM, DL-AP5 50μM, DNQX 20μM, TTX 0.1μM, 5%(v/v) FBS, 5%(v/v) Trehalose). Tissue pieces were dissociated into single-cell suspension by gently pipetting using Pasteur pipettes with polished tips. Cell suspension was passed through 40μm filter and centrifuged (200g) for 7min at 4℃. The precipitate was re-suspended in 1ml Drop-seq buffer. Cell concentrations were determined with hemocytometer and diluted into 150 cell/μl with Drop-seq buffer.
  - cDNA libraries were prepared using drop-seq protocols described by Macosko et al (2015).
Data processing:
  - Illumina bcl2fastq2 v2 software used for basecalling
  - Read 1 (21 bp long) contains both barcode and UMI. Bases 1-12 are cell barcode and bases 13-20 are UMI. Position 21 is usually a T and can be ignored.
  - sequenced reads were tagged with cell barcodes and molecular barcodes with Drop-seq tools v1.12, and aligned to GRCm38 whole genome with STAR, the final digital gene expression (DGE) matrix were generated by Drop-seq tools v1.12 as described by Macosko et al (2015).
  - The DGEs were filtered into STAMPs DGEs (single-cell transcriptomes attached to microparticles) by  dropbead (R package)
  - The STAMPs DGEs were normalized to their total reads counts
  - Genome_build: GRCm38
  - Supplementary_files_format_and_content: digital gene expression matrix
Relations:
  - BioSample: https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/biosample/SAMN09688207
  - SRA: https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/sra?term=SRX4403360
Supplementary files:
  - ftp://ftp.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/samples/GSM3290nnn/GSM3290582/suppl/GSM3290582_CT14.csv.gz
