Samples were homogenized using a dounce homogenizer in ice cold homogenate buffer. Homogenates were centrifuged at 900xg for 10 minutes and pellets were resuspended in homogenate buffer with 25% Optiprep solution. Samples were passed through a 40um cell strainer prior to FACS sorting.
